# 6 Original Dad Jokes - Scoring

## Joke 1: Home Improvement
**Joke:** "I hired a contractor to fix my door, and it was ajar."

- **Pun:** "ajar" sounds like "a jar" (also means the door is slightly open)
- **Unexpectedness:** 7/10 - clever homophone that catches you off-guard
- **Economy:** 8/10 - very concise at 10 words
